About MindBloom

Supporting Mental Agility with Dignity & Care

Designed specifically for senior citizens and individuals facing memory difficulties, especially focusing on communities across the North Eastern Region.

What is MindBloom?

MindBloom is a compassionate web application created for SIH 2026 (SIH26003). It provides stimulating brain exercises, daily routine reminders, and easy progress tracking to keep everyday life organized and enjoyable.

Who is it Designed For?

Elderly Individuals: Seniors experiencing mild memory slips or early dementia symptoms who benefit from structured mental activities.

Caregivers & Families: Relatives and healthcare aides who want gentle visibility into their loved one's daily engagement.

Why Cognitive Engagement?

Regular cognitive engagement helps maintain focus, recall, and confidence. By adapting difficulty automatically, MindBloom prevents frustration, encourages daily practice, and creates a positive, stress-free routine.

How MindBloom Works

A seamless, adaptive cycle designed to support elderly users at their own personal pace.

1
🎮

User Plays Game

The elderly user plays simple, enjoyable cognitive games like Memory Matching.

2
📊

Performance Recorded

Accuracy, response time, mistakes, and hints are captured automatically in the background.

3
🤖

AI Analyzes Data

A Random Forest machine learning model analyzes recent performance patterns.

4
⚙️

Difficulty Personalized

The next game is automatically calibrated to Easy, Medium, or Hard to keep play enjoyable.

5
📈

Progress Tracked

Caregivers and users view trends over time without any complex medical jargon.
